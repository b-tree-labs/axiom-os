# library.access

Who has access to one Library file or folder, and why (SHR-R3.1, R3.3). Not
your account's sign-in or site access.

A Viewer who is not on the list sees only the Owner and the general access line,
as in Google Drive; the Owner, Editors and the people listed see everyone.

Risk: `read`.

## Inputs

- `id`: a file id, or `folder:<path>`
